Problem Statement:

Ayan is planning to purchase a car on loan and needs to calculate the monthly instalment he 
needs to pay. You are required to implement a function, car_loan_installment, which calculates 
the monthly payment based on the loan amount, an annual interest rate, and the loan tenure. 
Use a global constant for the annual interest rate, and the function should return the monthly 
instalment amount.

Global Constants:

ANNUAL_INTEREST_RATE: A global constant representing the annual interest rate on the car loan.

(e.g., ANNUAL_INTEREST_RATE = 0.07 corresponds to an annual interest rate of 7%).

Function Signature:

car_loan_installment(loan_amount, loan_duration)

Input format :
The first line of input consists of a floating-point number loan_amount, representing the 
car's loan amount.

The second line of input consists of an integer loan_duration, representing the loan tenure in 
years.

Output format :
The output consists of a single line that states the calculated monthly instalment amount for 
the car loan, rounded to two decimal places, in the following format: "Monthly car instalment: 
{calculated amount}"
