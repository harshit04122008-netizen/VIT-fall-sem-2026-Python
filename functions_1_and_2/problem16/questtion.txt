Problem Statement:

Sarah works at a retail store and needs a program to help customers quickly calculate the 
total price of an item after adding sales tax. The store applies a fixed sales tax rate of 8% 
(0.08), defined as a global constant.

Write a recursive function that takes the item's cost as input, computes the sales tax, and 
returns the total cost. The program should then display the original cost, the tax rate, and 
the final total price to the customer.

Help Sarah provide accurate pricing with this simple calculator!

Function Signature:  total_cost(item_cost)

Input format :
The input consists of a single line containing a positive floating-point number representing 
the cost of the item.

Output format :
The first line of output prints "Item Cost: " followed by the item's cost formatted to two 
decimal places.

The second line prints "Sales Tax Rate: " followed by the sales tax rate as a percentage with 
a % symbol.

The third line prints "Total Cost: " followed by the total cost after applying sales tax, 
formatted to two decimal places.
