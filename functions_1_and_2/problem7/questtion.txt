Problem Statement:

Anya is planning to invest a sum of money and wants to estimate its future value after a 
certain number of years. She knows the initial investment, the annual growth rate, and the 
number of years the investment will grow.

Help Anya build a program that uses the built-in pow() function to calculate the final 
investment value. The result should be displayed rounded to two decimal places for clarity.

Formula: Final value of the investment = initial × (1+ rate)years

where;

initial - Initial investment amount

rate - Annual growth rate (expressed as a decimal)

years - The number of years the investment is held

Input format :
The first line consists of a floating-point number representing the initial amount of the 
investment.

The second line consists of a floating-point number representing the annual growth rate as a 
decimal.

The third line consists of an integer representing the number of years over which the 
investment will grow

Output format :
The output displays the final value of the investment after the specified number of years, 
rounded to two decimal places.
