Problem Statement



Ethan is curious about projecting his height from age 6 to 10 based on his measurements from birth to age 5. To accomplish this, he plans to write a program using NumPy to facilitate data manipulation. He intends to utilize the np.squeeze() function to remove single-dimensional entries from arrays during the process.



By incorporating a simple growth model with a 10% annual growth rate, Ethan aims to predict his height for the next five years. The program must collect Ethan's height measurements from age 0 to 5, process the data using NumPy, and print the projected heights for ages 6 to 10 as strings, rounded to two decimal places.



This program will enable Ethan to visualize his potential height progression over the coming years.

Input format :
The input consists of six lines, each containing a floating-point number, representing Ethan's height (in centimeters) from age 0 to age 5.

Output format :
The output should consist of exactly two lines:



Predicted height for ages 6 to 10:

['height_6', 'height_7', 'height_8', 'height_9', 'height_10']



The second line must be a list of strings, where each predicted height is rounded to two decimal places and enclosed in quotes.
